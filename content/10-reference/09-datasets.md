The examples on this site are built on a handful of toy datasets. Each is small enough to look at row by row, and each is generated from a fixed random seed, so every page sees exactly the same numbers. Wherever one appears, its card has the same four tabs: a **plot**, the **table** of raw values (with a CSV download), the **numbers** computed from it with what each one tells us, and a short **code** snippet that recomputes them.

Each card below lists every page where its dataset appears, in reading order.
